REVIEW NOTES

Background location — why it is required
Lociate's core function is notifying the user when they physically return to a place
where they previously left a voice note. This requires "Always" location
authorization: the geofence must fire while the app is not in the foreground, which
is the entire feature. The app requests "When In Use" first during onboarding and
only escalates to "Always" on the screen that explains proximity notifications. It
never uploads location for advertising or analytics.

How to exercise the core loop in the simulator
1. Launch, complete onboarding, grant location, microphone, and notifications.
2. Tap the record button, speak, and stop. The note saves with the current
   coordinate and an on-device transcription (Apple Speech; no network).
3. In Xcode, Debug > Simulate Location, choose a location several kilometres away.
4. Simulate the original location again. A proximity notification arrives within a
   minute; tapping it opens the note.

Subscriptions
Premium and Family are auto-renewing subscriptions with a 7-day free trial. The
paywall is reachable from Settings > Upgrade, and every gated feature shows an
upgrade prompt at the point of use. Free tier allows 10 loci; the limit is enforced
both on device and server-side.

Demo account
The demo account above is pre-seeded with loci and one household so Family sharing
can be reviewed without creating a second account. Sign in via Settings > Sign In.
The account password must be set before submission — see the placeholder in
demo_password.txt.

Privacy
Transcription is on-device via Apple Speech. Audio is encrypted at rest. No
tracking, no IDFA, no ad networks. Analytics are anonymous product-interaction
signals via TelemetryDeck with a SHA-256-hashed user identifier.
