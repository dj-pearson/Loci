Lociate turns the places you visit into a personal audio journal. Record a voice note, and Lociate pins it to that exact spot. When you return, it finds you — a gentle notification with the memory you left behind.

RECORD ANYWHERE, REMEMBER EVERYWHERE
Tap once to start recording. Lociate captures your voice, tags it with your GPS location, and transcribes it on-device — no internet needed, no cloud processing, completely private.

PROXIMITY NOTIFICATIONS
Walk past a coffee shop where you saved a note? Lociate quietly reminds you. The geofence triggers are powered by iOS, so they work without draining your battery.

FAMILY SHARING
Pin a note to grandma's house for your kids. Share grocery reminders at the store with your partner. With Family plans, up to 6 household members can share and discover each other's loci.

AI-POWERED CATEGORIES
Lociate automatically suggests categories for your notes — groceries, ideas, memories, tasks — so everything stays organized without effort.

MAP & LIST VIEWS
Browse all your loci on a beautiful map with color-coded pins, or switch to a searchable list view. Filter by category, search by keyword, or find notes near your current location.

PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
• All transcription happens on-device using Apple Speech
• Data stored locally by default — no account required
• Audio files encrypted at rest
• No tracking, no ads, no data selling

WORKS OFFLINE
Every feature works without internet. Cloud sync is optional and only available with Premium plans.
